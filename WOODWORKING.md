# Wood Finishing Guide

## Danish Oil Finishing Instructions

### Materials Needed
- Danish Oil
- Clean lint-free cloths
- Fine-grit sandpaper (220-320 grit)
- Protective gloves

### Instructions for Drawers and Frames
1. Sand the wood surface thoroughly
2. Clean all dust from the surface
3. Apply danish oil with a clean cloth
4. Let it soak for 15 minutes
5. Wipe off excess oil
6. Allow 24 hours to dry
7. Repeat for additional coats if needed

### Safety Notes
- Work in a well-ventilated area
- Dispose of oil-soaked rags properly to prevent spontaneous combustion
